Privacy Policy

1. Pass Delivery: Attendee contact details are used solely to issue BookMyShow/District Live passes.

2. Promoter Billing: Upfront net discounts (MRP − Commission %) are applied at checkout; banking PINs and sensitive financial credentials are never collected.

3. App Fulfillment: Passes are punched directly to customer accounts on BookMyShow or District Live.

4. DPDP Compliance: Tixora complies with India’s DPDP Act and never sells user data.

Terms of Service

1. Primary Passes: All passes come from authorized primary allocations. Commercial resale or markups are prohibited.

2. App Fulfillment: Passes are punched directly to the attendee's BookMyShow or District app account. Attendees enter using their official app.

3. Net Billing: Promoters pay the net discounted pass amount directly upon booking. Margins are retained upfront.

4. Cancellations: Cancelled shows receive a 100% face-value refund; postponed passes remain valid under BookMyShow rules.
