What is the Tixora Promoter Program?
Tixora connects official BookMyShow and festival ticket allocations with campus promoters, offering authentic passes and instant upfront promoter discounts.
How does promoter commission work?
Promoters receive instant upfront discounts (net billing) ranging from 8% to 16%. If MRP is ₹1,000 and your cut is 8%, you only pay ₹920 on the portal—retaining your margin upfront with zero payout delays.
Are passes purchased on Tixora genuine and official?
Yes. Every pass comes directly from BookMyShow or District Live official allocations with zero scalper markups.
How do attendees receive their tickets?
Once booked, official passes are punched directly onto the customer's BookMyShow, District, or event app account linked to their mobile number and email. Attendees enter using their official ticketing app.
Is there any deposit or fee to join as a promoter?
No. Joining requires zero deposits or fees, and verified promoters receive portal access immediately after KYC.
How are passes delivered after booking?
Confirmed passes go directly to WhatsApp and sync inside your BookMyShow or District Live ticket wallet.

Privacy Policy

1. Pass Delivery: Attendee contact details (name, mobile, email) are used solely to issue BookMyShow and District Live passes.

2. Promoter Billing: Upfront net discounts (MRP − Commission %) are applied at checkout; banking PINs and sensitive financial credentials are never collected.

3. Anti-Scalping: Security checks block scalper bots and duplicate passes.

4. DPDP Compliance: Tixora never sells or rents user data and complies with India’s DPDP Act.

Terms of Service

1. Official Passes: All passes come from official primary allocations. Secondary resale or markups are prohibited.

2. App Fulfillment: Passes are punched directly to the attendee's BookMyShow or District app account. Attendees enter using their official app.

3. Net Billing: Promoters pay the net discounted pass amount directly upon booking. Margins are retained upfront.

4. Cancellations & Rescheduling: Postponed passes stay valid for new dates under BookMyShow rules.
