Legal & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Version 2.4 • Effective November 2026 • Tixora Technologies Private Limited

01. Scope & Entity

This Privacy Policy explains how Tixora Technologies Private Limited (“Tixora”) collects and protects personal data across tixoralive.vercel.app.

02. Information We Collect

A. Attendee Pass Delivery

Full name, mobile number (WhatsApp & SMS alerts), email address, and BookMyShow or District pass details.

B. Promoter Verification & Payouts

Full name, college or city, WhatsApp contact, KYC proof, and UPI ID for commission payouts.

C. Anti-Scalping Security

Dynamic QR verification timestamps and device signals used solely to block ticket scalping and duplicate scans.

03. How We Use Data

  • Pass Delivery: Issue official passes to your BookMyShow or District ticket wallet.
  • UPI Payouts: Trigger instant UPI commissions after confirmed gate entry.
  • Anti-Scalping: Reject static screenshots and automated booking bots.
  • Tax & Compliance: Maintain TDS and banking audit records under Indian law.

04. Cookies & Local Storage

We use first-party session storage strictly for promoter login, referral attribution, and cart holds. We do not use third-party ad trackers or sell user data.

05. Infrastructure Partners

Data is shared only with authorized partners required to issue passes and process payouts:

  • Ticketing Partners: BookMyShow (Bigtree Entertainment Pvt. Ltd.), District Live, and event producers.
  • Payment Partners: Licensed Indian UPI payment gateways for promoter payouts.
  • Cloud Storage: Encrypted cloud databases with strict row-level access controls.

06. Security & Retention

All traffic uses TLS 1.3 encryption. Gate verification logs are purged after 180 days once post-event reconciliations close.

07. User Rights & Grievance Officer

Under India’s IT Act, 2000 and DPDP Act, 2023, you may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data.
