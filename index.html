Frequently Asked Questions

Protocol questions,
answered clearly.

Verification standards, primary ticketing allocations, Dynamic QR Mesh anti-scalping, and promoter UPI commission clearing.

What is the Tixora Promoter Protocol?
Tixora is an authorized primary live concert ticketing infrastructure and youth promoter protocol. It connects official stadium allocations from BookMyShow and festival partners directly to campus ambassadors and music community organizers, providing guaranteed authentic digital passes and automated under 60-second UPI commissions on gate verification.
How and when are promoter commissions paid?
Commissions are automated via our NPCI direct settlement bridge. Within 60 seconds of a pass being scanned and authenticated at the venue turnstiles, your performance cut (ranging from 8% to 16% based on your promoter tier) is deposited directly into your registered UPI ID with a real-time bank reference number.
Are passes purchased on Tixora genuine and primary?
Yes, 100%. Tixora operates strictly with authorized primary ticket engine allocations. Passes are cryptographically generated using our Dynamic QR Mesh Protocol and linked to BookMyShow or District Live wallet accounts with zero secondary marketplace scalping.
How does the Dynamic QR Mesh anti-scalping technology work?
Each issued pass features a time-seeded rotating digital signature that updates dynamically. Static screenshots, forward attempts, and screen recordings fail gate turnstile authentication, eliminating black-market ticket scalping and counterfeit fraud.
Is there any collateral, deposit, or sign-up fee to join as a promoter?
None whatsoever. There are zero upfront deposits, zero collateral requirements, and zero sign-up fees. Anyone accepted into the Tixora ambassador network receives a unique referral code and promotional toolkit immediately upon KYC verification.
How are passes delivered to attendees after booking?
Confirmed passes are instantly pushed to the attendee's verified mobile number via official WhatsApp notification and populated directly inside their BookMyShow or District Live ticket wallet for one-tap gate access.

Privacy Policy & Data Governance

1. Information Collection: Tixora collects necessary ticketing identity data (name, mobile number, and email) solely to generate authorized event admission passes and route verified dynamic barcodes via BookMyShow and partner venue turnstiles.

2. Promoter Settlement Data: For registered promoters, verified UPI handles and WhatsApp contact numbers are utilized strictly for automated performance payouts and direct KYC verification. Banking credentials and UPI PINs are never requested or stored.

3. Anti-Scalp Telemetry: Anonymized device integrity and rapid-request telemetry are analyzed during checkout to shield inventory from automated scalper bots and duplicate pass generation.

4. Third-Party Allocations: Tixora does not sell, rent, or trade customer data to advertising brokers. Ticket fulfillment complies with Indian Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act standards.

Terms of Protocol & Ticket Allocation

1. Official Primary Allocations: All passes issued via the Tixora mesh protocol are direct primary tier allocations from authorized event organizers. Secondary resale, commercial markup, and unauthorized ticket transferring are strictly prohibited.

2. Dynamic Turnstile Verification: Passes must be displayed on a live digital device at the venue gate turnstile. Static screenshots, photocopies, and forwarded images fail cryptographic verification and will be denied entry without refund.

3. Promoter Commission Settlement: Promoter commission yields are calculated based on confirmed venue gate verification. In the event of chargebacks, fraudulent bookings, or cancelled orders, unearned commissions are voided.

4. Event Rescheduling: In the event of artist cancellation or venue postponement by the festival producer, original passes remain valid for rescheduled dates in accordance with BookMyShow primary ticketing regulations.
