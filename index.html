Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions,
answered clearly.

How does the Tixora Promoter Network work?
Tixora connects primary BookMyShow and festival ticket allocations with campus promoters, offering authentic passes for fans and upfront net discounts when booking on the portal.
How does promoter commission work?
Promoters receive instant upfront discounts (net billing) ranging from 8% to 16%. If MRP is ₹1,000 and your cut is 8%, you pay only ₹920 on the portal—keeping your margin immediately with zero payout delays.
Are passes purchased on Tixora genuine and primary?
Yes. Every pass comes directly from BookMyShow or District Live inventory at official face value.
How do attendees receive their tickets?
Once booked, official passes are punched directly onto the customer's BookMyShow, District, or event app account linked to their mobile number and email.
Is there any collateral, deposit, or sign-up fee to join as a promoter?
No. Joining is completely free with zero deposits, and verified promoters receive portal access immediately.
How are passes delivered to attendees after booking?
Confirmed passes go directly to WhatsApp and sync inside your BookMyShow or District Live ticket wallet.
