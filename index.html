Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions,
answered clearly.

How does the Tixora Promoter Network work?
Tixora connects primary BookMyShow and festival ticket allocations with campus promoters, offering authentic passes for fans and under-60s UPI payouts when buyers scan in at the venue.
How and when are promoter commissions paid?
Within 60 seconds of a gate scan, your 8%–16% commission is sent straight to your registered UPI ID.
Are passes purchased on Tixora genuine and primary?
Yes. Every pass comes directly from BookMyShow or District Live inventory at official face value.
How does Dynamic QR anti-scalping protection work?
Each pass displays a refreshing QR code that blocks screenshots and unauthorized forwards at venue gates.
Is there any collateral, deposit, or sign-up fee to join as a promoter?
No. Joining is completely free with zero deposits, and verified promoters receive portal access immediately.
How are passes delivered to attendees after booking?
Confirmed passes go directly to WhatsApp and sync inside your BookMyShow or District Live ticket wallet.

Privacy Policy

1. Pass Delivery: Attendee contact details are used solely to issue BookMyShow/District Live passes.

Terms of Service

1. Primary Passes: All passes come from authorized primary allocations. Commercial resale or markups are prohibited.

2. Gate Entry: Show live Dynamic QR passes on your phone at entry. Screenshots or forwards fail verification without refund.
